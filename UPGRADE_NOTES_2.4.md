# Voice of Goila 2.4 — Mobile-first motion pass

Visual changes are intentionally scoped to category pages and lightweight home-card feedback. No Supabase, authentication, review, submission, admin, PWA or APK logic was edited.

- Distinct purple/white Home button with visible border and depth.
- Add Info button has slow periodic shimmer and breathing shadow, including on touch-only devices.
- Subcategory cards now have clearly visible pale brand tints and purple/gold top accent.
- Staggered entrance, touch press feedback, softer cards, decorative hero highlight.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` disables movement.

Test on a staging deployment before production; live database and PWA integrations have not been exercised here.
